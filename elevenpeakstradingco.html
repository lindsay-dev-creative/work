It Started With One Good Dog

Eleven Peaks Trading Co. was founded in 2014 by a small group of Boykin Spaniel owners who couldn't find apparel and gear that celebrated their favorite breed the way it deserved. So we made our own.

What began as a few hand-printed tees has grown into a full line of apparel, headwear, pet gear and gifts — all designed in-house and built to hold up in the field, on the water and at home on the couch.

We're proudly independent, obsessively detail-driven, and we donate a portion of every sale to Boykin Spaniel rescue and health research.
